Why Current Systems Fail

Four systemic failures driving the crisis

Zero-Label Identification

Barcodes degrade on decommissioned parts. Workers can't identify whether a valve is 316L SS (₹142/kg) or carbon steel (₹25/kg).

Hidden Safety Hazards

Asbestos, lead solder, pressurized components & hydrocarbon residue remain invisible — until a worker is hurt.

Lost Recovery Value

Mixing 316L Stainless Steel with carbon steel erases 83% of scrap value. India loses crores annually from misidentification.

No Bin Intelligence or Traceability

Collection trucks run fixed routes — visiting empty bins and missing full ones. No IoT monitoring, no audit trail, no compliance records.
